Mac Apps

Free tools I built for macOS. Download, unzip, and follow the README inside.

Media Downloader
Version 1.0

A Mac app with a built-in browser for saving the images and videos on any web page.

  • Finds every image and video on the page
  • Download one item or a whole batch
  • Video sites and streams through yt-dlp

Requires

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or newer
  • Apple Silicon or Intel
  • Optional: brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg for video sites

Discord Splitter
Version 2.2 beta

Splits big videos into parts that fit under your Discord upload limit and play right inside Discord.

  • Drag and drop a queue of videos
  • Lossless split, or shrink with a quality check
  • Presets for free, Nitro Basic and Nitro limits

Requires

  • macOS 13 Ventura or newer
  • Apple Silicon or Intel
  • Installer sets up ffmpeg with Homebrew

Procon2 KBM
Version 1.0

Use a Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller in PC games that only support keyboard and mouse. Built and tuned for Rust.

  • Sticks and buttons become keys and mouse movement
  • Control panel for sensitivity, dead zones and profiles
  • Runs in the background, starts at login

Requires

  • macOS 13 Ventura or newer
  • Apple Silicon or Intel
  • Switch 2 Pro Controller over USB-C
  • The procon2 tool

Opening the apps

These apps aren't from the App Store, so the first time you open one macOS will say it can't check it. To open it anyway:

  1. Try to open the app or Install.command once, and close the warning.
  2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click Open Anyway.
