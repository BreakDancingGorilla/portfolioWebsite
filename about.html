About me

I'm a self-taught developer. I started teaching myself to code in my junior year of high school and have been building ever since: browser games, websites, Discord bots and 3D projects.

Most recently I was a Kodak inkjet technician at RR Donnelley, setting up and troubleshooting variable-data print systems on million-piece press runs. Now I'm focused on my own projects and freelance work while I look for my next role. I like making things people can open and use right away, whether that's a game in the browser or a tool that saves someone time.
